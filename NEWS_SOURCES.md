26 Nov 2026 in any time zone. The Kuwait drone attack, the ECB choice
and the Gaza strikes all happened on Thursday 26 Nov. The US tariff
proposal, the Netanyahu hearing and the Solomon Islands treaty were
announced on Wednesday 25 Nov (Canberra/Washington time) and dominated

without a date claim inside the image beyond the masthead date. The
Cairo tanker fire broke out on Wednesday evening, 25 Nov, and the
